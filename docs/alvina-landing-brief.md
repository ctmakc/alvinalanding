## Best-Use-Case Patterns Chosen

- Hero with founder photo + clear local positioning
- Immediate split by audience intent: buyers vs sellers
- Guide-first lead capture instead of generic contact-first UX
- Authority layer built from market-update content and public education
- Multi-channel trust layer: Instagram, YouTube, TikTok, LinkedIn
- Direct contact options visible near the CTA and again in the footer/contact block

## What The New Landing Must Do

- Show Alvina's face in hero and keep the page unmistakably personal-brand-led
- Make Ottawa the center of the positioning, not generic Canada-wide copy
- Support multilingual UX with English, Russian and French
- Surface seller value proposition without making the whole brand look discount-driven
- Turn the buyer guide and seller guide into primary conversion assets
- Reinforce that Alvina is active, modern and content-driven across platforms

## Information Architecture

1. Hero
2. Brand/content signal strip

4. Market-authority cards

## Visual Direction

- Editorial heading type, warmer body typography
- Palette: deep navy, warm ivory, gold, subtle wine accent
- High-end but approachable, not cold luxury
- Soft glass/paper cards over a warm atmospheric background
- Strong mobile readability and obvious CTA hierarchy

## Offer / CTA Stack

- Book a strategy call
- Request buyer guide
- Request seller guide
- Call or text directly
- Send short inquiry form

## Content Notes

- Keep copy strategic and easy to scan
- Prefer clarity over hype
- Avoid generic realtor cliches unless attached to a concrete benefit
- Use public facts only; do not invent achievements or certifications
